## Software bundled with EasyPrint

### OrcaSlicer

EasyPrint includes **OrcaSlicer 2.5.1**, which prepares models for the printer. It is licensed under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0** (AGPL-3.0).

- OrcaSlicer is shipped **unmodified**, as a separate program that EasyPrint runs. It is not linked into EasyPrint.
- Its licence text is installed with it, in the EasyPrint program folder under `resources\slicer\engine\LICENSE.txt`.
- Its source code is available at [github.com/OrcaSlicer/OrcaSlicer/tree/v2.5.1](https://github.com/OrcaSlicer/OrcaSlicer/tree/v2.5.1).

The Elegoo Centauri Carbon print settings that EasyPrint uses come from OrcaSlicer's Elegoo profiles. They are included under the same AGPL-3.0 licence, with no values changed.

### Fonts

The font used for words in models is **Droid Sans**, copyright The Android Open Source Project, licensed under the **Apache License 2.0**. Its notice is installed in the EasyPrint program folder under `resources\licences`.

## Downloaded on request

The **built-in designer** for *Describe what I want* is not included in the installer. When an administrator downloads it, EasyPrint fetches a **Qwen3.5** model (Standard 4B or Large 9B), licensed under the **Apache License 2.0**.

## Online services

EasyPrint connects to Neon (database and accounts), and optionally to Anthropic (Claude designs) and Thingiverse (model search). Each service has its own terms, which apply to your use of it. If you add an Anthropic API key, your use of Claude is billed and governed by Anthropic.

## Models from Thingiverse

Models imported from Thingiverse belong to their creators and keep their own licence, often a Creative Commons licence. EasyPrint records each model's licence and author when it is imported. Check the licence before printing a model for sale or sharing it, as some licences do not allow commercial use.
