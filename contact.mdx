## Who to contact

| You are | Contact first |
| --- | --- |
| **The person who prints** | Your support person. They set up EasyPrint for you and can fix most problems. |
| **A support person or administrator** | North Byte Tech, by email, when [troubleshooting](/admin/maintenance#troubleshooting) does not solve it. |
| **Asking about licences or buying EasyPrint** | North Byte Tech, by email. See [Licensing](/licensing). |

## What to include

So we can help quickly, please include:

- what you were doing and what happened,
- the date and time it happened,
- the EasyPrint version (in **App settings**, under **Updates**),
- the relevant lines from the **Activity log** in the administration area, or the log files in `%APPDATA%\EasyPrint 3D\logs`.

<Note>EasyPrint hides passwords and keys in its logs automatically, so the log files are safe to send.</Note>

<Warning>Never send your database connection string, Anthropic API key or administrator password by email.</Warning>
