EasyPrint 3D is a Windows program that lets someone with no 3D printing knowledge make real objects on an **Elegoo Centauri Carbon** printer. It takes care of the difficult parts: getting the model ready for the printer, checking it fits, sending it over the network, and following the print until it is finished.

EasyPrint has two sides:

- **The everyday side** is for the person who prints. Large, calm, step-by-step screens, no password. They pick or make a model, choose a colour and size, and press **PRINT**.
- **The administration side** is for their support person. Signed in with email and password, it controls which models, colours and materials are available, sets safety rules, approves prints, and connects the printer.
