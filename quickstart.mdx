This page is for the support person who installs EasyPrint. No programming is needed. It takes about 15 minutes.

## What you need

| Item | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| A Windows 10 or 11 PC | 64-bit. 8 GB memory is fine; 16 GB if you want the large built-in AI designer. About 1 GB of disk, plus 3–6 GB for the optional built-in AI designer. |
| An Elegoo Centauri Carbon | On the **same network** as the PC (Wi-Fi or cable). |
| Internet access | People, the model library and print history are stored online. |
| The installer | `EasyPrint3D-Setup.exe`, from whoever supplies EasyPrint to you. |
| Connection details (sometimes) | Only if your copy of EasyPrint asks for them during setup. |

## Set up EasyPrint

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the program">
    Run **EasyPrint3D-Setup.exe**. If Windows shows _"Windows protected your PC"_, press **More info** and then **Run anyway** (the installer is not yet code-signed). Choose a folder (the default is fine), tick **Desktop shortcut** if you want one, press **Install**, and leave **Run EasyPrint 3D** ticked when you press **Finish**.

    You do not need Windows administrator rights. EasyPrint installs for the current Windows user only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put the printer on the network">
    On the printer's touch screen, connect it to your Wi-Fi or plug in a network cable. Note its **IP address** from the printer's network settings (for example `192.168.1.42`).

    <Tip>
      Give the printer a fixed address in your router so it does not change.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start setup">
    The first time EasyPrint opens it shows **Set up EasyPrint 3D**. It checks it started properly. Press **Start setup**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect online services (only if asked)">
    Some copies of EasyPrint already include their connection and skip this step. If it appears, paste the **Database connection string** (starts with `postgresql://`) and the **Neon Auth URL** from whoever supplied EasyPrint. The **Anthropic API key** is optional; see [AI designs](/admin/ai-designs). Press **Check and save**. The details are stored encrypted on this PC.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your administrator account">
    Enter your name, email and a strong password. Only the first administrator is created here; you can add more later in **People and accounts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the printer">
    EasyPrint searches the network for the Centauri Carbon. Choose it, or type its IP address. EasyPrint connects and shows the printer's status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the slicer">
    EasyPrint prepares a small test cube to prove its built-in slicing engine works.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish">
    Choose **Start EasyPrint**, **Add models** or **Go to administration**. From now on, EasyPrint opens on the everyday side and asks the person who prints for their name. They do not need a password.
  </Step>
</Steps>
