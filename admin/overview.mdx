## Sign in

On the everyday side, open **My settings** and press **For your support person: administration** at the bottom, then sign in with your email and password. While you are signed in, an **Administration** button also appears at the top of every everyday screen.

If you forget your password, press **I forgot my password** on the sign-in page and enter the 6-digit code that is emailed to you. Another administrator can also set a new password for you.

## Everyday

| Page | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | What is happening now: the printer, prints waiting for you, recent activity. |
| **Prints to approve** | Approve a print, or reject it with a short note the person sees. |
| **Printer** | Connection (search again or change the IP address), what the printer is doing, camera on or off, light. |
| **Print history** | Every print, who printed it, how long it took and how it ended. |

## Library

| Page | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Models** | Upload an STL or 3MF, give it a name, description, category, sizes and allowed colours, then **publish** it. Only published models appear to the person printing. |
| **Find on Thingiverse** | Search the free Thingiverse library and import models. EasyPrint records each model's licence and author. |
| **Categories** | Rename, reorder or add library categories. Ten are created for you. |

## People

**People and accounts** has three parts:

- **People** are the names typed in on first start. For each person, choose whether their prints need approval, whether they can upload models or use **Describe what I want**, or **pause** them (they then see a friendly paused screen).
- **This computer** shows which person this PC belongs to. **Forget this computer** makes EasyPrint ask for a name again on next start. The old person's prints and models stay.
- **Administrators** lets you add administrators, reset their passwords or remove them.

## Printing

| Page | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Colours and materials** | The spools you own. Mark colours as available and which one is loaded. Each colour belongs to a material: PLA, PLA+, PLA Matte, PLA Silk or PETG. |
| **Safety and permissions** | The [safety rules](/admin/safety-rules). |
| **Print quality settings** | Advanced: adjust slicer values on top of the built-in Centauri Carbon presets. |

<Warning>Leave **Print quality settings** alone unless you know what each value does.</Warning>

## System

| Page | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **App settings** | [AI designs](/admin/ai-designs), Thingiverse, starting accessibility settings for new people, [updates](/admin/maintenance#updates), the online services connection, and **Run setup again**. |
| **Activity log** | Everything that has happened: prints, approvals, setting changes, problems. Look here first when something goes wrong. |
