A 3D printer builds a real, solid object out of plastic, one thin layer at a time. EasyPrint does the difficult parts for you. Someone who looks after the computer for you, EasyPrint calls them your **support person**, has already set everything up.

## The first time you open EasyPrint

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open EasyPrint 3D">
    Open it from the desktop or the Start menu. A short loading screen appears while it starts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell EasyPrint your name">
    EasyPrint asks **What is your name?** Type your name and press the button. You do not need a password. EasyPrint remembers you from now on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The home screen

The home screen asks *What would you like to do today?* and shows four big buttons:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Print something** | Pick a ready-made model from the library and print it. |
| **Create something** | [Make your own model](/guides/create-a-model). |
| **My prints** | See what you have printed and anything printing now. |
| **My favourites** | Models you have saved with the heart button. |

If something is printing, a box at the top shows its name, how far along it is and how long is left. Press it to see more.

## Print a library model

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a model">
    Press **Print something**, choose a category (for example *Animals*, *Toys* or *Keychains*), then choose a model.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Colour">
    Choose one of the colours your support person has made available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Size">
    Choose **Small**, **Normal** or **Large**. Sizes that are too big for the printer are marked and cannot be chosen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check">
    Look at your model and turn it around to see all sides. EasyPrint may suggest **supports** (a temporary scaffold for parts that hang in the air) or a **brim** (a thin rim that helps the model stick to the printer), and explains why. Usually, keep what it recommends.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Print">
    EasyPrint gets the model ready, which takes a few seconds to a few minutes. If asked, check the printer bed is empty, tick the box, then press **PRINT**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Your support person may want to check some prints first, for example very long ones. The screen then says *Waiting for your support person to approve it*. The print moves on once they approve it.
</Info>

## While it prints

Open **My prints** and choose the print to see how far along it is, the time left, the current layer, and a live picture from the printer's camera (if your support person has turned it on). You can press **Pause**, **Continue** or **Stop** at any time.

When it is finished, EasyPrint tells you with a message on the screen, and a sound if you have sound turned on. Wait a few minutes for the model to cool before taking it off.

<Note>You can only have one print on the way at a time. Finish or cancel it before starting another.</Note>

## Safety

<Warning>
  Do not touch the nozzle or the bed while printing or just afterwards: they are hot. Keep the printer door closed while it prints. If anything looks or smells wrong, press **Stop** in EasyPrint or on the printer, and tell your support person.
</Warning>
