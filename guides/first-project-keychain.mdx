You will make a flat keychain with your name on it, using **Build with shapes**. It takes about 10 minutes to make, then the printer does the rest.

## You will use

* A **Block** – the flat tag.
* **Words** – your name.
* A **Ring** – the loop for the key ring.

## Steps

### 1. Start a new creation

1. On the home screen press **Create something**.
2. Press **Build with shapes**.

You see an empty printer bed and the buttons for building.

### 2. Make the tag

1. Under **Add a shape**, press **Block**.
2. The block is chosen in **Your shapes**. Under **Size**:
   * press **Wider** a few times, so it is long like a tag,
   * press **Shorter** until it is thin and flat.

### 3. Add your name

1. Under **Add a shape**, press **Words**.
2. In the **Words** box, type your name.
3. Under **Size**, press **Smaller** or **Bigger** until the name fits on the tag.
4. Under **Move**, use **Left**, **Right**, **Back** and **Forward** to put the words in
   the middle of the tag.
5. Press **Up** until the words stick out of the top of the tag a little. They should
   still touch the tag – not float above it.

### 4. Add the loop

1. Under **Add a shape**, press **Ring**.
2. Press **Smaller** until it is about the size of a key ring hole.
3. Under **Move**, press **Left** (or **Right**) until the ring is at one end of the tag,
   half on and half off. It should lie flat, like the tag.

<Note>**Made a mistake?** Press **Undo**. Press **Redo** to bring it back.</Note>

### 5. Choose a colour to look at

Under **Preview colour**, pick a colour. This is only for looking at it. You choose the
real plastic colour when you print.

### 6. Save it

1. Under **Save**, type a **Name**, for example *My keychain*.
2. Press **Save my creation**. EasyPrint shows **Saved**.

### 7. Print it

1. Press **PRINT**.
2. Choose a **colour**, then a **size** (Small is good for a keychain).
3. Look at the **check** screen, then press **PRINT** again.

See [After your print finishes](/guides/after-printing) for taking it off the printer.

## Tips

* Keep the tag **flat and thin**. Flat things print quickly and stick well.
* Every shape must **touch or overlap** the tag. A shape that floats on its own prints as
  a separate loose piece, or not at all.
* Short names look best. For long names, make the tag wider.
* Your keychain is saved in **My models**. You can change it or print it again any time.
