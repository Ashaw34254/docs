Press **Create something** on the home screen. You may see up to four choices; your support person decides which ones you can use.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Build with shapes" icon="box">
    Put blocks, balls, cylinders, cones, rings and words together on a virtual printer bed. Move them, make them bigger or smaller, and change their colour. When you are happy, save it and print it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Describe what I want" icon="message-square">
    Type what you would like in your own words, for example *a keychain with my name Sam* or *a small plant pot*. EasyPrint designs a model you can change with shapes or print straight away.

    - Many common ideas (keychains, name signs, phone stands, plant pots, simple toys) are made instantly from ready-made designs.
    - For other ideas EasyPrint may use an AI designer. If it cannot do everything you asked, it tells you what it left out.
    - There may be a limit on how many AI designs you can ask for each day.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Upload a model" icon="upload">
    If you already have a 3D model file, an **STL** or **3MF** file (for example from a website), upload it. EasyPrint checks it can be printed and that it fits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My models" icon="folder">
    Everything you have made or uploaded before. Print it again, change it or delete it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

When your model is ready, printing it follows the same steps as a [library model](/guides/print-a-model#print-a-library-model).
