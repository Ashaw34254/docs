### Can I close EasyPrint while it prints?

**Yes, once the printer has started printing.** The printer keeps going on its own. When
you open EasyPrint again, it checks with the printer and shows how the print went.

**Do not close EasyPrint while it is getting your model ready or sending it to the
printer.** If you do, EasyPrint says *EasyPrint was closed while getting this print ready*
and you need to start that print again.

### Should I leave the printer on all the time?

No. **Switch it off when you are not using it.** It is safer and saves power. Switch it
on about a minute before you want to print; EasyPrint finds it by itself.

Before switching off, wait until it has cooled (the nozzle temperature on its screen is
below about 50 °C). Never switch it off while it is printing or heating up. See
[Turning the printer on and off](/guides/print-a-model#turning-the-printer-on-and-off).

### How big are Small, Normal and Large?

* **Normal** is the size the model was designed at.
* **Small** is half that size.
* **Large** is one and a half times that size.

Some library models have their own sizes chosen by your support person. If a size is too
big for the printer, EasyPrint marks it and you cannot choose it.

### How long will my print take?

EasyPrint shows the **print time** before you press PRINT, and the **time left** while it
prints. Small, flat models can take 15 to 30 minutes. Big or tall models can take many
hours. To make it quicker, choose a smaller size or **Quick** quality.

Very long prints may need your support person to approve them first.

### Why is it taking so long to get ready?

EasyPrint is working out every layer for the printer. Big or detailed models take
longer, sometimes a few minutes. You can wait on the screen, or go and do something else –
EasyPrint tells you when it is ready.

### Can I print the same thing again?

Yes. On a finished print, press **Print it again**. You can also find your past prints in
**My prints**, and things you made in **My models**.

### Can I print two things at once?

No. You can have **one print on the way at a time**. Finish or stop it first.

### Can I save a model for later?

Yes. On a library model, press **Save** (the heart) to add it to **My favourites**.
Things you make or upload are kept in **My models**.

### What if the power goes off while printing?

The printer stops. When the power comes back:

1. Look at the printer's own screen and follow what it says.
2. Open EasyPrint. It checks the printer and shows what happened to your print.
3. If the print did not finish, take what was printed off the plate (once it is cool) and
   start again.

Tell your support person it happened.

### What if I chose the wrong colour or size?

If the print has not started yet, press **Stop** and start again with the right choice.
If it is already printing, you can stop it, but the plastic used so far is wasted.

### The print looks wrong (stringy, messy or not sticking). What do I do?

Press **Stop**, wait for it to cool, clear the plate, and tell your support person. They
may need to clean the plate or check the printer.

### Why can I not see "Upload a model" or "Describe what I want"?

Your support person has turned them off for you. Ask them if you would like to use them.

### Do I need a password?

No. EasyPrint remembers you on this computer. Only support people sign in.
