EasyPrint always tells you in plain words what has happened and what to do.

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *The printer is not connected. Check it is switched on.* | Make sure the printer is on and on the same Wi-Fi or network as the computer. |
| *The printer is busy.* | Wait for the printer to finish what it is doing. |
| *Please check the printer bed is empty first.* | Remove anything left on the printer, then tick the box. |
| *That size is too big for the printer.* | Choose a smaller size. |
| *You already have a print on the way.* | Finish or cancel that print first. |
| *EasyPrint cannot see the printer right now.* | The printer lost contact while printing. Go and check on it. The buttons on the printer's own screen always work. |
| *You have made a lot of models today.* | You have reached today's limit for AI designs. Try tomorrow, or build one with shapes. |
| *EasyPrint has been paused by your support person.* | Ask your support person. |

For anything else, ask your support person. They can see the activity log and fix most problems. Support people can find more help in [Administration troubleshooting](/admin/maintenance#troubleshooting).
