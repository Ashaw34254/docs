## Before you start

**Picture:** a finger pressing the printer's power switch.

Switch the printer on.

**Picture:** a clock.

Wait 1 minute.
EasyPrint finds the printer by itself.

**Picture:** an empty printer plate.

Make sure there is nothing on the printer plate.

---

## Print something

**Picture:** the EasyPrint icon on the desktop.

1. Open **EasyPrint**.

**Picture:** the "Print something" button.

2. Press **Print something**.

**Picture:** a list of categories.

3. Choose a group. For example **Animals**.

**Picture:** a model.

4. Choose a model you like.

**Picture:** colour circles.

5. Choose a **colour**.
   Press **Next**.

**Picture:** Small, Normal and Large buttons.

6. Choose a **size**.
   Press **Next**.

**Picture:** the model turning on the screen.

7. Look at your model.
   Press **Next**.

**Picture:** the "bed is empty" tick box.

8. Check the printer plate is empty.
   Tick the box.

**Picture:** the big green PRINT button.

9. Press **PRINT**.

---

## While it prints

**Picture:** the progress bar.

The screen shows how much is done.

The screen shows how long is left.

**Picture:** a hand near a hot nozzle with a cross.

Do not touch the printer while it prints.
It is **hot**.

**Picture:** the Pause and Stop buttons.

To stop for a moment, press **Pause**.
To stop for good, press **Stop**.

---

## When it is finished

**Picture:** a happy tick.

EasyPrint plays a happy sound.
The screen says **Your model is finished!**

**Picture:** a clock.

Wait 10 minutes.
The printer needs to cool down.

**Picture:** hands lifting the plate out.

Lift the plate out.

**Picture:** hands bending the plate.

Bend the plate a little.
Your model pops off.

**Picture:** the plate going back in.

Put the plate back in the printer.

**Picture:** the printer screen showing the temperature.

Wait until the printer is cool.
The number on its screen should be under 50.

**Picture:** a finger pressing the power switch off.

Switch the printer off.

**Picture:** a hand on the switch with a cross.

Never switch it off while it is printing.
