Press **My settings** at the top of the screen. Changes are saved straight away.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Colours** | Light, dark, or follow the computer. |
| **High contrast** | Stronger colours and outlines. |
| **Text size** | Normal, large or extra large. |
| **Reduce motion** | Fewer animations. |
| **Simplified screens** | Less text on each screen. |
| **Guided Mode** | One question and four big answers at a time. |
| **Spoken instructions** | EasyPrint reads instructions out loud. |
| **Sounds and messages** | A sound and message when a print finishes or has a problem. |

Your support person chooses the starting settings for new people.

## Advanced printer controls

If your support person allows it, an **Advanced** button at the top opens advanced controls: temperatures, moving the print head, fans and the light, print speed, the files stored on the printer, and the printer's history.

<Warning>Most people never need the advanced controls. If you are not sure, leave them alone.</Warning>
