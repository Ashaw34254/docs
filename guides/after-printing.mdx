When your model is finished, EasyPrint plays a short happy sound (if sounds are on),
shows **Your model is finished!** and, if spoken instructions are on, says it out loud.

## 1. Let it cool

The nozzle and the plate are still hot when the print finishes.

* **Wait about 5 to 10 minutes** before you touch anything.
* Bigger models need longer. If the model still feels warm, wait a bit more.
* Keep the printer door closed while it cools.

<Note>Cool plastic comes off the plate more easily. Pulling a warm model can bend it.</Note>

## 2. Take the plate out

The Centauri Carbon has a thin, bendy metal plate that sits on magnets.

1. Open the printer door.
2. Hold the plate by its front edge and **lift it straight out**.
3. Put it on a table.

## 3. Take the model off

1. **Gently bend the plate** a little, like bending a ruler. The model usually pops off
   on its own.
2. If it does not, bend it the other way, or bend a different corner.
3. Do not use a knife or scraper on the plate. If the model will not come off, ask your
   support person.

## 4. Remove supports and brim (if your model has them)

* **Brim** – the thin flat rim around the bottom. Peel it away with your fingers, like
  peeling a sticker. Small bits can be snapped off.
* **Supports** – the thin scaffolding under parts that hang in the air. Wiggle them and
  snap them off a piece at a time. Small pliers help.

<Warning>Supports and brims can have sharp edges once they are snapped off. Ask your support person to smooth sharp edges if you need it.</Warning>

Put the leftover plastic in the rubbish (or a recycling box if your support person has
one).

## 5. Leave the printer ready for next time

1. Make sure **nothing is left on the plate**: no bits of brim, no stringy plastic.
2. Put the plate back in the printer. It snaps onto the magnets. Make sure it sits flat
   and lines up with the back of the printer.
3. Close the door.

Next time you print, EasyPrint will ask you to check the bed is empty. Because you
cleared it now, you can just tick the box.

## Print it again

On the finished print's page, press **Print it again** to make another one. You can
also find it later in **My prints**, or in **My models** if you made it yourself.
