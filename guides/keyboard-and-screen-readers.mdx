You can do everything in EasyPrint without a mouse or touch screen.

## Keys

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Tab** | Move to the next button, box or choice. |
| **Shift + Tab** | Move back to the previous one. |
| **Enter** or **Space** | Press the button you are on. |
| **Arrow keys** | Move between choices in a group, for example between colours, sizes or settings like **Text size**. The choice you move to is selected. |

The first time you press **Tab** on a screen, a **Skip to main content** link appears.
Press **Enter** to jump past the buttons at the top, straight to the main part of the
screen.

## Moving through the steps

When you print, EasyPrint goes step by step (**Colour**, **Size**, **Check**, **Print**).
Each time you go to a new step, EasyPrint moves to the heading of that step, so you always
start at the top and hear what the step is.

The list of steps at the top says which step you are on and which ones are done.

## Screen readers

EasyPrint works with screen readers such as **Windows Narrator** (press
**Windows + Ctrl + Enter** to turn it on) and **NVDA**.

* Every button and picture has a name the screen reader can read.
* Important messages, such as *Your model is finished!* or an error, are read out as soon
  as they appear.
* Groups of choices (colours, sizes) are read as a group, with how many choices there
  are.
* Buttons that cannot be pressed yet say why, for example *Choose a colour first.*

## Spoken instructions without a screen reader

If you do not use a screen reader but would like EasyPrint to talk, turn on **Spoken
instructions** in **My settings**. See [Sounds and messages](/guides/sounds-and-messages).

## Making it easier to see

In **My settings** you can:

* make text **large** or **extra large**,
* turn on **high contrast**,
* choose **light** or **dark** colours,
* turn on **reduce motion** to stop animations.

You can also use Windows' own zoom: press **Ctrl** and **+** to make everything bigger,
**Ctrl** and **-** to make it smaller, and **Ctrl** and **0** to go back to normal.
