EasyPrint uses three short sounds. You can turn sounds on or off in **My settings**
(**Sound and messages**).

| Sound | What it means | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **One short note** | Something moved on. Nothing is wrong. | *Your model is prepared. You can press PRINT now.* Also when a print is paused, a design is ready, or a creation is saved. |
| **Three notes going up** (happy) | **Your model is finished!** | Your print has finished. Wait for it to cool, then take it off. |
| **Two notes going down** | **Something needs attention.** | *Your print stopped.* Or something you tried did not work. EasyPrint shows what happened on screen. |

## Messages on the screen

When a print finishes or stops, EasyPrint also shows a message in the corner of the
computer screen (a Windows notification), even if EasyPrint is hidden behind another
window.

* **Your model is finished!** – go and get your model (after it cools).
* **Your print stopped** – read the message. It says why and what to do.

You can choose in **My settings** whether to get a message when a print finishes and when
there is a problem.

## Spoken instructions

If **Spoken instructions** is on, EasyPrint reads out:

* the title and first sentence of each screen when it opens,
* *Your model is prepared. You can press PRINT now.*,
* *Your model is finished!* and the model's name,
* *Your print stopped* and the reason.

Turn up the computer's volume if you cannot hear it.
