**Describe what I want** turns your words into a real model you can print. Here is how
to get what you want the first time.

## What works straight away

EasyPrint has ready-made designs for these. They are made instantly, every time:

| | | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| keychains | signs with words | coasters |
| medals | hearts | cat, bear and mouse faces |
| pen holders | plant pots | boxes |
| phone stands | rings | dice |
| a snowman | a rocket | a tree |
| a house | a toy car | a robot |

For other ideas, EasyPrint may use an AI designer (if your support person has turned one
on). AI designs take a little longer and work best with simple ideas.

## How to say it

**Say what it is, then add details.**

| Instead of… | Try… |
| --- | --- |
| *a present for my mum* | *a heart keychain that says MUM* |
| *something for my desk* | *a pen holder* |
| *a sign* | *a sign that says SAM'S ROOM* |
| *a cool animal* | *a cat face* |

### Words EasyPrint understands

* **Words to put on it** – put them in the description: *a keychain with my name Aroha*,
  *a sign that says WELCOME*.
* **Colour** – *red*, *blue*, *green*, *yellow*, *orange*, *purple*, *pink*, *white*,
  *black*, *grey*, *brown*, *gold*, *silver*, *teal*, *navy*. This changes the colour you
  see on screen; you choose the real colour when you print.
* **Size** – *tiny*, *mini*, *small*, *little*, *big*, *large*, *huge*, *giant*.

### Good examples

* *a small blue keychain with my name Sam*
* *a big plant pot*
* *a coaster with a heart on it*
* *a sign that says KEEP CALM*
* *a tiny robot*
* *a phone stand*

## Keep it short and simple

* One thing at a time. *A rocket* works better than *a rocket, a moon and three stars*.
* A few words are enough. You can type up to about 600 letters, but short is better.
* Simple shapes print best. Very small details (like tiny eyelashes) may not show.

## If it is not quite right

* EasyPrint tells you if it left something out.
* Press to change it with shapes: you can make parts bigger, move them, or add words.
  See [Make a name keychain](/guides/first-project-keychain).
* Or try again with different words.

<Note>There may be a limit on how many AI designs you can ask for each day. Ready-made designs (the list above) do not count towards the limit.</Note>
